Why it matters

771 million people live without clean water.

That's nearly 1 in 10 people on the planet. Every day, women and children walk an average of 3.7 miles to collect water that often isn't even safe to drink — time that could be spent in school, at work, or simply being kids.

charity: water brings clean, safe drinking water to people in developing countries, funding sustainable projects that communities can rely on for decades. When a well is built, everything changes: health improves, girls stay in school, and families build a future.
